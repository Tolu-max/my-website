TConnect — Fintech VTU & Payment Engine

Architected end-to-end fintech platform automating telecom airtime, mobile data, and utility bill settlement for Nigerian consumers.

  • Atomic Wallet Ledgers: Implemented row-level locking in MySQL to guarantee zero double-spending during concurrent purchase spikes.
  • Payment Reconciliation: Dual gateway integration (Paystack & Monnify) with cryptographically verified webhooks for immediate transaction settlement.
  • Production Scale: Successfully processed 3,000+ live customer transactions with full automated auditing.
